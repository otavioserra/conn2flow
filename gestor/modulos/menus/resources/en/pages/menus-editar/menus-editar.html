Menu Availability

Build the menu structure by adding items of different types. Drag by the handle to reorder; drag right/left to nest as a child or move back to the previous level (WordPress style). Click an item to select it: the next item is inserted right below it.

Menu content

The HTML below is the template rendered on the site. Use the <!-- item < --> ... <!-- item > --> block for leaf items and the <!-- item-parent < --> ... <!-- item-parent > --> block for items with a submenu — inside it, the [[item#children]] placeholder receives the recursive render of the children. Per-item variables: [[item#label]], [[item#url]], [[item#slug]] and [[item#css_classes]]. Use <!-- no-item < --> ... <!-- no-item > --> for the empty state.

# 2.9.39

The floating Live Editor toolbar arrived on published pages, with live DOM mapping and protection against accidental clicks. Contextual panels insert elements, widgets, forms, and embeds; session templates and backups support review and restoration. AI assistance uses CodeMirror, and custom-code editing covers HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and extra head content. See [widgets](../concepts/widgets.md) and [CSS and Tailwind](../concepts/css-and-tailwind.md).
